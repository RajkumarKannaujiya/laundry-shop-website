A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

A neat finish starts with the right conversation.

Steam pressing is a practical choice for clothes that need a clean, pressed finish. Tell us what you are bringing—everyday wear, office clothes or a special outfit—and we can explain the available service for that garment.

Fabric, trims and care-label instructions matter. If an item is delicate, embellished or has a special finish, mention it before service so we can check what is suitable. For sarees, ask us about saree roll press as a separate option.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Share the garment type and fabric, if known.
  • Point out delicate details or areas that need attention.
  • Ask for the current rate and expected readiness time.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
