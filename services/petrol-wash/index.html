A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

A specialist service needs a quick check first.

Some garments require a specialist cleaning method because of their fabric, construction or decorative details. The correct process depends on the care label and the item itself; a name alone does not determine whether the service is appropriate.

Before arranging petrol wash, tell us what the garment is made of, whether it has embellishments and if it has been treated at home before. We will confirm whether we can accept it and explain the next step. Do not attempt solvent cleaning at home.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Call with the garment type and care-label instructions.
  • Mention embroidery, trims, stains and previous treatments.
  • Confirm acceptance, rate and turnaround before handing it over.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
