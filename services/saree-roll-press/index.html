A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Every saree has its own care story.

Sarees vary in weave, weight, borders, embellishments and care instructions. Share what you know about the fabric and tell us whether the saree has been cleaned or treated recently. We can check whether roll pressing is suitable for the item.

For silk, delicate weaves or heavily embellished sarees, it is especially helpful to check the care label and discuss the garment before service. Finishing preferences, price and timing should be confirmed with us before drop-off.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Mention the saree fabric and any care-label instructions.
  • Tell us about zari, embroidery, trims or delicate borders.
  • Ask about finishing, current price and expected readiness.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
