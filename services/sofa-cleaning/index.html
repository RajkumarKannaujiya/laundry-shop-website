A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Furniture needs a different kind of care.

Sofa materials, construction and cleaning instructions vary. Share the upholstery type if known, the number of seats, visible marks and your area so the team can check whether the service is available for your furniture.

Ask what preparation is needed, how pricing is calculated and what timing is possible. Do not assume that an upholstery cleaning method is suitable for every fabric or leather finish.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Share the upholstery material and approximate sofa size.
  • Mention stains, odours or delicate details.
  • Confirm location coverage, price and service timing before booking.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
