A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Start with the stain and fabric details.

Stains can react differently depending on the fabric, dye, stain age and cleaning products already used. Explain what caused the mark, when it happened and whether you have tried to remove it at home.

A cleaning assessment can help identify a suitable next step, but no process can promise that every stain will disappear. Call before visiting so we can confirm current availability and advise what details to share.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Name the stain if you know what caused it.
  • Mention the fabric and any care-label instructions.
  • Tell us about home remedies or previous cleaning attempts.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
