A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Start with the care label. We’ll take it from there.

Dry cleaning is used for garments whose care instructions call for a non-water cleaning process. Suiting, formalwear and occasion garments can have different fabrics, linings and trims, so suitability should be checked item by item.

Tell us about stains, embellishments, previous home treatment and any time you need the garment back. We can explain the available process and expected timing before you decide. Cleaning results vary by fabric and stain history, so no stain-removal outcome should be assumed in advance.

Before you bring your clothes

  • Check and share the garment care label.
  • Point out stains, embellishments or earlier stain treatments.
  • Confirm the price and expected readiness time before booking.

Prices and turnaround depend on the item and service. Call us for current details before drop-off.
